Try it here: [coachella.kylehe.com](https://coachella.kylehe.com)

This is a web app I built to solve a very specific problem: coordinating a Coachella weekend with a big group of friends is a mess. Everyone has different must-see sets, the lineup spans multiple stages at once, and half the time is spent walking across the polo fields from one stage to the next.

So I made a shared planner where a group can build out their weekend together and actually see where everyone is headed.

The app worked great and the weekend was super fun!
